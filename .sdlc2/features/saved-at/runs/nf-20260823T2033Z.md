**Feature:** saved-at — Stamp every saved name with when it was saved, and show it in the list.

screen is open, not freeze at whatever it said on render. Sorting newest-first should be
available, a name should carry a marker when it is the most recent one, and names older than a
day should fall off the list. Deliberately unsettled: when the same name is saved again, does its
stamp refresh to now or keep the original save time — and whichever it is, that answer has to
hold for sort order, for the newest marker, and for the day-old cutoff at the same time.
